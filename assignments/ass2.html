Motor Vehicles in the Indian Market

Scooter

Scooters are lightweight two-wheelers suitable for daily commuting. Popular brands include Honda Activa, TVS Jupiter and Suzuki Access.

Motorcycle

Motorcycles are available in commuter, sports and cruiser models. Popular brands include Hero, Bajaj, Royal Enfield and Yamaha.

Sedan

Sedans provide comfortable seating and good boot space. Popular models include Honda City, Hyundai Verna and Maruti Ciaz.

SUV (Sport Utility Vehicle)

SUVs are preferred for their powerful engines, spacious interiors and high ground clearance. Popular models include Tata Nexon, Mahindra Scorpio and Hyundai Creta.

Why Buy Any Vehicle?

Vehicles provide comfort, save travel time and make daily commuting easier. They are useful for both personal and professional purposes.
